Dates & sources

Apps use the source publication time; lectures and patents use their recorded dates. Date-only records use midnight in Korea. Year-only records have no invented precise timestamp. Undated records follow dated records. Rebuilding does not change IDs or publication times.

The original source of lecture and research records is bhban.kr. Check publication and preprint labels, citations and original links. Public API access does not change the original documents’ terms of use.

All files are updated during the site build. Depending on hosting, JSON Feed may use application/json, which JSON Feed 1.1 permits.
